05 / THE GAS GIANT

Discover Jupiter.

Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system. This enormous gas giant has colourful cloud bands, powerful storms and a fascinating family of moons.

View All Planets

King of the planets.

Jupiter is made mostly of hydrogen and helium. It has no solid surface like Earth. Its atmosphere contains swirling clouds and the famous Great Red Spot, a giant storm that has lasted for centuries.

POSITION

5th planet

DAY LENGTH

About 10 hours

YEAR LENGTH

11.86 Earth years

KNOWN MOONS

Many

Did you know?

Four of Jupiter's best-known moons are Io, Europa, Ganymede and Callisto. Ganymede is larger than the planet Mercury.
