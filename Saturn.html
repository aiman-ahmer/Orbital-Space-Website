06 / THE RINGED GIANT

Meet Saturn.

Saturn is a giant planet famous for its magnificent ring system. Its rings contain countless pieces of ice and rocky material orbiting the planet.

View All Planets

A masterpiece of the cosmos.

Saturn is mostly made of hydrogen and helium. Although its rings look solid from a distance, they are made of many separate particles ranging from tiny grains to much larger chunks.

Did you know?

Saturn's moon Titan has lakes and seas of liquid methane and ethane on its surface. Scientists are interested in studying Titan's unusual environment.
