08 / THE OUTER SOLAR SYSTEM

Journey to Neptune.

Neptune is a distant, deep-blue ice giant. It lies far from the Sun and is known for its extremely fast winds and dynamic atmosphere.

View All Planets

At the edge of our solar system.

Neptune is the eighth planet from the Sun. Because of its great distance, it receives much less sunlight than Earth. Its atmosphere contains hydrogen, helium and methane.

POSITION

8th planet

DAY LENGTH

About 16 hours

YEAR LENGTH

165 Earth years

KNOWN MOONS

16

Did you know?

Neptune has incredibly fast winds. Its largest moon, Triton, orbits the planet in the opposite direction to Neptune's rotation.
