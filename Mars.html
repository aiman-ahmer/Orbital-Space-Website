04 / THE RED PLANET

Explore Mars.

Mars is a cold, rocky desert world known for its reddish appearance. Scientists study it to understand its ancient environment and whether it once had conditions suitable for life.

A planet of possibilities.

Mars has giant volcanoes, deep valleys and polar ice caps. Robotic rovers explore its surface, collecting information about its rocks, climate and history.

Did you know?

Mars is home to Olympus Mons, one of the largest known volcanoes in the solar system. Its two small moons are called Phobos and Deimos.
