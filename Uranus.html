07 / THE ICE GIANT

Discover Uranus.

Uranus is a distant ice giant with a pale blue-green appearance. It has faint rings, many moons and an unusual rotation that makes it seem to spin on its side.

View All Planets

The sideways planet.

Uranus contains substances such as water, ammonia and methane in its deep interior. Methane in its atmosphere absorbs red light, contributing to its blue-green colour.

POSITION

7th planet

DAY LENGTH

About 17 hours

YEAR LENGTH

84 Earth years

KNOWN MOONS

Many

Did you know?

Uranus has an extreme axial tilt of about 98 degrees. As a result, its seasons are unusual, and parts of the planet can experience very long periods of daylight or darkness.
