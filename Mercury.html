01 / THE INNER SOLAR SYSTEM

Meet Mercury.

The smallest planet in our solar system is also the closest to the Sun. Its rocky surface is covered with craters from ancient impacts.

View All Planets

A world of extremes.

Mercury has almost no atmosphere to hold heat. Because of this, its days can be extremely hot while its nights become very cold. Despite being close to the Sun, it is not the hottest planet.

Did you know?

Mercury has a very large iron-rich core compared with its size. Its surface looks somewhat like Earth's Moon because both are covered in impact craters.
